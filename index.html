SYNTHIA — Understanding financial markets

A financial data and intelligence platform · Built with C++

Financial information comes from many sources and often arrives in different formats. SYNTHIA is being built to bring those observations together, track where they came from, and make market information easier to inspect and replay consistently. In simple terms: a foundation for turning scattered financial data into a more coherent picture of the world.
